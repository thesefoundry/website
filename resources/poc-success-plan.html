POC & POV Success Plan Template

A working document, not a polished one. Built to be filled out with your prospect, not handed to them after the fact. This page gives you both halves of the loop: a template to build the plan with and an AI coach to pressure-test it while the POC is still young enough to change.

Most proof-of-concepts fail quietly. Nobody declares them dead. They just drift: the trial period expires, the champion goes quiet, and everyone moves on without ever agreeing on what a win would have looked like. This template exists to stop that from happening.

Use this in two steps

Build the plan with your prospect, then get it reviewed while every gap is still a cheap conversation. A plan review in week one costs you a phone call. The same findings at the readout cost you the quarter.

  1. Build the plan at kickoff with the template. Open it on the kickoff call and fill in every section with your prospect, live.
  2. Run it through the POC Plan Coach. A free Claude skill built from how I review POC plans. Within a few days of kickoff, it scores the plan, checks your own launch read, and ranks the fixes by deal impact.

What's inside the template

The document is built around seven sections, and you fill in every one of them with your prospect, not for them.

  • Objective: the problem statement and business cost driving the evaluation, so everyone stays anchored to why this matters
  • Project participants: named people and roles on both sides, including an explicit executive sponsor slot, so that question gets asked early instead of late
  • Success criteria: a table of specific, testable criteria with a yes/no outcome column, not vague goals
  • Timing: start and end dates, budget availability, and your technical meeting cadence
  • Project scope: the product-specific boundaries of what's actually being evaluated
  • Procurement: EULA review, InfoSec requirements, purchase order approval, and partner vs. direct
  • Support: where the prospect sends issues during the evaluation, and who else should be looped in

How to use it

Open this at kickoff, not after. Walk through it live with your champion and, if you can get them in the room, their economic buyer. The value isn't the document. It's the conversation it forces you to have before either side has sunk real time into the evaluation.

The POC Plan Coach: an AI coach built from how I coach

The template gives you the structure. The skill gives you the coach. I turned the way I review POC plans into a skill Claude can run: evidence-cited scores across nine dimensions, from value stance and the executive sponsor to success criteria and the business next step, every genuine strength named, and the fixes ranked by deal impact, each one a concrete change you can make this week on this POC.

It also checks your own read. Before you launch, you make the call: run it, fix first, or don't run it. The coach tells you whether the plan backs that up. Sometimes the most valuable POC is the one you talk the customer out of.

It won't grade what it can't see. A plan still full of placeholders gets coached as exactly that, and criteria the customer dictated while you took notes don't count as criteria you shaped. It also looks past the obvious sections. Your timing and procurement answers are MEDDPICC in disguise, and the coach reads them that way.

Here's the power move: bring the kickoff transcript along with the plan. The coach treats the tape as ground truth. It checks who actually proposed each success criterion, whether the customer agreed to the final list out loud, and whether the asks got made: the sponsor ask, the give-get, and the if-then next step. The plan shows what got written down. The tape shows who committed to it.

Using your own template? Expected. The coach scores the intent behind each dimension wherever it lives in your document, and it will tell you when a gap is your template's fault instead of yours. No plan yet? Use the same rubric as a pre-flight before kickoff.

Installing it takes about a minute:

  1. Download the .skill file below.
  2. Open Claude (web or desktop), go to Settings, find Skills under Capabilities, and upload the file. Claude Code users can unzip it into ~/.claude/skills instead.
  3. Start a chat and say "review my POC plan." Attach your completed plan and the kickoff transcript. The coach takes it from there.

After the review, take it up on the next rep. It will play your champion while you practice the sponsor ask or the scope trade out loud, draft the customer conversation that closes the gap it found, and hold you to a re-review at your mid-POV checkpoint.

It runs in your Claude, on your data. Nothing comes back to me unless you send it. Works best on Claude Sonnet or better.
