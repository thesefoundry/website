Discovery Call Review Worksheet

Everyone reviews the demo. Almost nobody reviews the call that decided what the demo should be. This page gives you a full coaching loop for discovery: a worksheet to score against and an AI coach to score with.

Discovery is where deals actually get qualified. It's also where most SEs get the least feedback. The typical review is "good call, lots of info," which tells you nothing about whether the customer named their own pain, whether you asked what happens if they do nothing, or who did most of the talking. (Spoiler: on the calls that go sideways, it's usually the SE.) This worksheet swaps the gut feel for a rubric, and the coach holds you to it.

Use this in two steps

Score your own call before anyone else does. Self-critique is a skill, and the SEs who improve fastest are the ones who build it. Then let the coach check your work.

  1. Self-assess with the worksheet. Listen to your recording with the worksheet open, score all seven dimensions, drop timestamps as you go, and make your deal read: lean in, not yet, or walk.
  2. Run it through the Discovery Call Coach. A free Claude skill built from how I coach discovery. It scores the call, compares its read to yours, and coaches on the gap.

What's inside the worksheet

One page to fill out while you listen, plus a checklist you can run on your own between coaching sessions.

  • The scorecard: seven dimensions, from structure and the Up-Front Contract to call control and next steps, each scored 1 to 5 with room for notes and timestamps
  • The high-leverage question check: the handful of questions that do most of the qualifying work, including "What would disqualify a vendor?" and "What happens if you do nothing?", and whether each one actually got asked
  • The deal read: your call on lean in, not yet, or walk, with the evidence from this call to back it up
  • The coaching conversation: one strength to reinforce, one focus for next time, and a re-review date so the focus actually gets practiced
  • An SE self-audit checklist: eight questions to run on your own recordings, from "Did the customer state the pain in their words, or did I state it for them?" to "Did I talk less than the customer?"

How to use it

Listen back within a day or two, while you still remember the room. Score as you go, and drop a timestamp the moment something jumps out, good or bad. A timestamp beats a vague memory, and it's what lets you show, not just tell, when you sit down to coach or be coached. Make the deal read before you close the worksheet. If you can't say lean in, not yet, or walk, and back it with evidence from the call, that's your first finding.

The Discovery Call Coach: an AI coach built from how I coach

The worksheet gives you the rubric. The skill gives you the coach. I turned the way I review discovery calls into a skill Claude can run: evidence-cited scores across all seven dimensions, every genuine strength named, a verdict on your own lean in, not yet, or walk read, and exactly one focus for next time, with a concrete fix you can drill. Not a pile-on. Not a compliment sandwich. A coaching conversation.

It won't grade what it can't see, and it won't count a pain you proposed and the customer nodded along to as a pain the customer stated. That one stings the first time. It's also the single most common discovery mistake I see.

Here's the power move: bring the transcript along with your worksheet. The coach treats the tape as ground truth. It checks your self-scores against what actually happened, quotes the moments that matter, checks which of the high-leverage questions really got asked, and estimates the talk ratio. On discovery, the talk ratio alone is worth the price of admission. And the price of admission is free.

No worksheet yet? Just tell it you had a first call with a prospect and want coaching. It'll walk you through a guided debrief, or send you back to the recording first if the call isn't fresh.

Installing it takes about a minute:

  1. Download the .skill file below.
  2. Open Claude (web or desktop), go to Settings, find Skills under Capabilities, and upload the file. Claude Code users can unzip it into ~/.claude/skills instead.
  3. Start a chat and say "review my discovery call." Attach your worksheet and the transcript. The coach takes it from there.

After the review, take it up on the next rep. It will play the customer while you practice your focus out loud, draft the follow-up email that closes any open loop it found, and hold you to your re-review date. Coaching without a next rep is just a review.

It runs in your Claude, on your data. Nothing comes back to me unless you send it. Works best on Claude Sonnet or better.
